How to play

Pick a tool from the bar at the bottom, then click your dummy. Everything runs on real physics.

Left clickUse the selected tool
Right dragOrbit the camera (or Alt + drag)
WheelZoom in and out
1 – 0, −, =Switch tools
SpacePause and resume
RReset every dummy
FSelect the next dummy
COpen the character creator
TouchOne finger uses the tool, two fingers orbit and pinch to zoom
